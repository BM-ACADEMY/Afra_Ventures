Products

What we have built, and how far along each one is.

Three products, three different stages. The label on each is the one we would give you on the phone.

01

GymDesk

Beta · in testing

Gym management software covering members, attendance, plans and packages and payments — with renewal reminders and enquiry follow-up that send automatically, and AI-assisted workout and diet plans. Separate roles for the platform administrator, gym owner, branch sub-admin and member. We are selecting pilot gyms now.

→ 02

Nera

In development

WhatsApp automation for businesses that lose customers to a slow reply. Answers every incoming message in seconds, runs follow-up sequences for anyone who goes quiet, and sends campaigns on Meta-approved templates over the official API. Built in two halves, inbound first.

→ 03

Velai Vaaippu

Live

A jobs platform serving job seekers, employer companies and college placement cells. Placement tracking for every student, company invitations and drive management from one panel. Free tiers for seekers and small employers, paid plans above that.

What the labels mean

Live, beta, in development.

These three words do a lot of work on this site, so here is exactly what we mean by each of them.

Live

You can use it now

The product is in production, people outside our team use it, and it is supported. Sign up and start.

Beta

Built, being tested

Every screen works and the product does the job end to end, but it has not yet run a full year of real operations. Pilot customers get it free and get our attention in return.

In development

Still being written

The design is settled and the code is underway. Nothing to sell yet. We publish it here so you can see the direction and tell us if we have it wrong.
