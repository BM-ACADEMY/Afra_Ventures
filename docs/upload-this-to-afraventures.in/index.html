How we work

Four things we decided early.

We build where the customer is

Our team sits in Puducherry, and the first users of everything we make are within driving distance. A gym owner can tell us the software is wrong to our face. That shortens the distance between a complaint and a fix to about a day.

One codebase per product

Every product is multi-tenant from the first commit. We do not fork a copy per customer, because a hundred forks is a hundred things to patch when something breaks.

Automation before headcount

Reminders, follow-ups, document generation and reconciliation run on workflow automation rather than on somebody remembering. It is how a team of twelve keeps several products moving at once.

We publish the stage

Live, beta, in development. Every product on this site carries its real label, and the label changes when the product does — not when it would be convenient.

Common questions

Before you write to us.

What does Afra Ventures do?

We build software products. Three of them are ours — GymDesk for gym operations, Nera for WhatsApp automation, and Velai Vaaippu for jobs and campus placement. We also take on custom product engineering for businesses and institutions that need software built properly and kept running.

Where is Afra Ventures based?

Our registered office and our development team are both at 78 Lenin Street, Kosapalayam, Puducherry 605013. We work with customers across India, and remotely where that suits the project.

Is Afra Ventures a new company?

The company was incorporated on 12 September 2026 under CIN U62011PY2026PTC009819. The products it runs were built by the same team over the preceding years and now sit under the company.

Is Afra Ventures a women-led company?

Yes. Both directorships and the majority shareholding are held by women, and that is the structure the company was set up with rather than one added later.

Can you build something for us?

Often, yes — if the problem is operational and a product could serve more than one customer, it is the kind of work we do well. Tell us what breaks today and what it costs you when it does. If we are the wrong people for it we will say so in the first conversation.

Have a problem worth solving?

Tell us what it costs you today. We will tell you honestly whether software fixes it.
